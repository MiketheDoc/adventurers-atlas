Rules and limitations

Spell slots follow the 2024 multiclass rules, with half-caster rounding up; Eldritch Knight and Arcane Trickster use the one-third multiclass contribution. Warlock Pact slots are a separate pool. Prepared spell level is calculated from each class independently. Entered class milestones are representative, not the entire feature text. Magus, Monster Hunter and Gunslinger are selectable but require source-specific mechanics not yet incorporated in the slot calculator.

Reference: D&D Beyond 2024 multiclassing rules.

How to use the Atlas

The Atlas compares power (combat impact, defence, support and sustained effectiveness) with versatility (how many different situations a build can solve). All 251 subclasses have a first-pass review and a position on the map. Scores are comparative guide ratings, not official Wizards of the Coast rankings or measured win probabilities.

Class mode averages the listed subclass scores with equal weight; it summarises this catalogue, not an independently measured base class. Zoom changes the scale without changing any score. The level slider uses simple indicative adjustments across levels 5–12 rather than a complete character simulator.

Understanding the six dimensions

Damage · Defence · Control

How a subclass hurts enemies, survives attacks and prevents enemies from acting effectively.

Support · Utility · Endurance

Healing and buffs; skills, travel and social tools; and staying useful across several encounters without rests.

Sources and editions

Only 2024/5.5e entries and officially supported 2024-compatible partner adaptations are listed; the older version of a republished subclass is not duplicated. Each subclass profile provides its source and a link where available. Partner content may have additional details in paid books, so consult the linked rules before using a feature at the table.

The Golden Tides profiles include known current abilities and selected future progression. A character's future spell choices are suggestions, not assertions that the player has prepared them. All power numbers are intended for comparison and discussion, rather than exact tactical forecasts.
